DAMAGED OR BROKEN PRODUCT?

Report a delivery issue.

If your Alpona product arrives broken or damaged, submit a claim here with valid evidence. You do not need to send the broken product back.

Damage Claim Policy

  1. A continuous unboxing video is required. Start recording before opening the package, then keep recording while you open the box and show the product clearly.
  2. The video must show the package before opening, the opening process, and the condition of the product when first revealed.
  3. A video recorded only after the product is already opened or broken does not meet the required evidence standard.
  4. Photos may be uploaded as additional evidence, but photos alone are not enough for a damage claim.
  5. Alpona reviews each claim. If the required evidence is valid and the claim is approved, Alpona will refund the eligible amount.
  6. No return shipment is required for an approved damaged-item claim. Keep the damaged item unless Alpona specifically contacts you with different instructions.
  7. Alpona does not offer general change-of-mind returns or exchanges. This form is for genuine damaged/broken product issues.
